# 1. GGUF Inference Engine Routing (`src/aqruntime/runtime/inference.py`)

- `InferenceEngine.generate(prompt)` checks:
  If `isinstance(self.model, GGUFModelWrapper)` or `isinstance(self.model, SimulatedGGUFModel)` or `getattr(self.model, "is_gguf", False)`:
  Routes to `_generate_gguf(prompt)`.

- `_generate_gguf(prompt)`:
  - Formats messages with chat template.
  - Calls `self.model.generate_text(prompt)` or `self.model.llm(formatted_prompt)`.
  - Appends to conversation history and saves memory session cleanly.
